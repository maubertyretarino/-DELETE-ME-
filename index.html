Welcome to Dibby!

Dibby is a simple web application that allows users to create and share their own digital drawings. With a user-friendly interface and a variety of drawing tools, Dibby makes it easy for anyone to express their creativity and share their artwork with friends and family.

Feature Description
Drawing Tools Dibby offers a variety of drawing tools, including pencils, brushes, and erasers, allowing users to create detailed and vibrant artwork.
Sharing Options Users can easily share their drawings with friends and family through social media platforms or by generating a unique link.
User-Friendly Interface Dibby's intuitive design makes it accessible for users of all ages and skill levels to create and enjoy digital art.
